Clinical Focus

Doctor Consultation Suite

Built to empower doctors to focus on patient diagnosis rather than typing notes.

Doctor Experience
1

Real-Time Patient Calling & Queue Sync

The doctor's queue automatically updates in real time as front desk registers patients. When the doctor clicks "Call Patient", the status updates simultaneously in the doctor portal, receptionist screen, and clinic waiting monitors from Waiting $\rightarrow$ Consulting.

2

Interactive 4D FDI Dental Charting (32 Teeth)

For Dental practitioners, MediTrack features an FDI two-digit world-standard dental chart (Upper Right, Upper Left, Lower Left, Lower Right). Doctors tap individual teeth to assign conditions (Caries, Missing, Crown, Root Canal) and select clinical procedures that automatically tally procedure billing.

3

Smart Prescription Builder & Dosages

Prescribing takes seconds with the smart medicine catalog. As the doctor types, autocomplete suggests brand names and generic strengths. Doctors pick dosage patterns (1-0-1, 1-0-0, 0-0-1), duration (e.g. 5 Days), and timings (After food / Before food).

4

One-Click Patient Medical History

Instantly view prior consultations, previous prescriptions, recorded vitals (BP, Pulse, Temperature, SpO2, Weight), and past diagnoses. Past medical history opens instantly in a compact modal without leaving the current consultation.

Front Desk & Finance

Smart Reception & GST Invoicing

Accelerate patient check-in, eliminate billing errors, and automatically dispatch invoices.

Receptionist & Accounts
A

Auto Gender Detection from Patient Name

Receptionists register patients in under 10 seconds. MediTrack automatically infers gender based on patient name recognition, eliminating manual toggles while allowing quick adjustments when needed.

B

End-to-End Status Lifecycle Synchronization

When consultation completes in the doctor portal, patient status transitions instantly to Billing (Unpaid) on the receptionist desk. Once payment is recorded, status transitions to Paid and Completed, with doctor consultation fees credited immediately to revenue analytics.

C

Dual UPI QR & Cash Payment Collection

Support instant UPI dynamic QR codes (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and cash collection. MediTrack keeps an exact daily cash counter split vs digital payments for painless end-of-day bank reconciliations.

D

Automated WhatsApp PDF Invoice Dispatch

No expensive thermal paper or printer jams. Upon payment, MediTrack automatically generates an official GST Tax Invoice PDF with clinic letterhead, consultation charges, procedure fees, and prescribed medicines, sending it straight to the patient's WhatsApp.
